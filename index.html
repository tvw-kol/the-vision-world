01 — THE STUDIO

An optical experience built around you.

The Vision World is a boutique optical studio in the heart of Minto Park, Kolkata. We combine a carefully curated selection of eyewear with practical optical expertise — helping you find the right fit, lens and look.

From everyday prescription glasses to statement eyewear, prescription sunwear and contact lenses, our approach is simple: listen first, recommend thoughtfully and make the finished pair feel like it was made for you.
